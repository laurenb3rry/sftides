Two constraints found in the font files. Your four faces contain exactly 63 glyphs — A–Z a–z 0–9 and space. There is no period, colon, comma, degree sign or middle dot, so 4.3, 61.2° and slack 11:42 cannot be set purely in your hand. Every frame below sets letters and digits in your writing and lets the punctuation fall through to the printed face — it reads as intentional, and it's the same thing you'd do in SwiftUI. Add those four marks to the font and it becomes seamless.

All four faces also report identical vertical metrics (x-height 0.48 em), so the correct small-size multiplier can't be computed from the files. It's a knob instead: each frame states its face and its multiplier so you can pick by eye.
